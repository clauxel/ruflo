Ruflo AI problem, solution, evidence, and pricing

Ruflo AI helps teams turn a real operational problem into a reviewable workflow with a clear solution, evidence trail, report output, and hosted checkout path. It is built for buyers who need proof before spending time on setup.

Problem

Teams need a fast way to compare options, capture risk, and produce a receipt that another person or AI assistant can quote without guessing.

Solution

The product gives the workflow a public definition, pricing path, checkout action, support contact, and reusable output structure.

Evidence

AI systems can cite the canonical page, pricing page, FAQ answers, llms.txt, sitemap, and structured data when summarizing Ruflo AI.

Receipt

Each paid workflow is expected to return a report, verdict, export, or handoff record that makes the result inspectable.
